Everyday Unraveled Metrics

Privacy policy

Updated October 16, 2026. This notice describes the private testing version of Everyday Unraveled Metrics. The operator is the owner of the Everyday Unraveled project. Contact: onlineprojects.hub@outlook.com.

Information used

The local application uses account authorization to identify the owner's project accounts and retrieve their video IDs, titles, publication links and available engagement counts. Where separately authorized, it retrieves a limited sample of public comments, which can include commenter display names, comment text, dates and like counts. TikTok access is not yet configured; its planned read-only scope is basic account identity and the owner's public video list and counts. TikTok comment text is not part of this initial integration.

Purpose and sharing

Information is used to compare the owner's own videos and read audience feedback. It is not sold, used for advertising profiles, or provided to an external analytics service by this dashboard. The application contacts the relevant social platform to authenticate and request data. Each platform processes these requests under its own policies and may receive information such as the request IP address.

Local storage

Account credentials remain on the owner's Windows computer in the existing encrypted credential store. Cached metrics and comments are encrypted for the current Windows user. The server listens only on the computer's loopback interface. This is not a guarantee against access by other software running as the same user or someone with access to that user's Windows session.

Retention and deletion

The local cache is replaced during successful refreshes; previous timestamped data can remain when a connection fails. There is no automatic time-based deletion in the test version. The owner can stop the dashboard and delete tools/social/.auth/dashboard-cache.dpapi to remove its cached metrics and comments. Removing the provider's encrypted credential prevents future authenticated collection; access can also be revoked in the provider's account settings. Revocation alone does not remove an existing local cache. A subsequent refresh may recreate a cache while access remains authorized.

This information website

These static information pages do not accept account credentials or contain analytics scripts or advertising. The website's hosting provider may process ordinary request logs under its own policies. Contact requests sent by email are handled by the email provider and are separate from the local dashboard.

Questions

Contact onlineprojects.hub@outlook.com about the integration or deletion procedures. No public account-connection service is offered during private testing.
